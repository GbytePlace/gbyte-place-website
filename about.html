Who we are

Your partner in people
and everyday delivery.

Gbyte Place Management Services is based in Lagos, Nigeria. We provide outsourcing services and managed remote teams for businesses that need capable people and practical operational support.

Our work spans customer service, virtual assistance, cybersecurity, data science and analytics, technology, and floorplan and virtual-tour production.

We combine recruitment with onboarding coordination, training support and performance management. The goal is to help people become a dependable extension of the client’s business.
